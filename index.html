What is Arduino Camp?

  • A one week camp for students looking to learn engineering basics
  • For students 6th grade and above
  • Assumes no prior coding experience
  • Maximum of 10 stuents

What will you learn about?

  • Gain hands-on experience with Arduino hardware and software.
  • Gain hardware skills like building circuits, designing schematics, and wiring electronics.
  • Learn to apply C++ in the Arduino development software to program your creation.
  • Build their own project tailored to solving a real-world problem, and present it to the class.
