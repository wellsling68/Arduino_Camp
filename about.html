About

Are you ready to dive into the fascinating world of electronics and coding? Our Arduino Engineering Camp is the perfect place for young innovators to explore their passion for technology and engineering. Here are some of the awesome things you will learn at this camp.

Collaborate with Peers
- Gain valuable hands-on experience by working together to tackle challenges
Learn to solve real-world problems
- Develop leadership and communication skills through engaging, practical activities
Take home your creation!
- Apply what you’ve learned to create a unique project that addresses an everyday problem, and bring home your creation at the end of the week
Gain valuable engineering skills!
- Learn circuit building and programming with the Arduino microcontroller Join us for an exciting journey of innovation and teamwork, and make a real impact!
